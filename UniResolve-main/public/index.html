How it works

Every complaint follows a clear, tracked path.

1. Submit

Choose a category and department, describe the issue, and attach proof if you have it. You can submit anonymously.

2. Reviewed & assigned

An admin verifies the complaint, sets its priority, and assigns it to the coordinator of the responsible department.

3. Resolved & rated

Track progress live, get notified on every change, then rate the resolution — or request a reopen if the issue returns.
